\section{Problem}

\begin{frame}{1. Project Goal}
    \textbf{Goal:} build a self-implemented multitask BERT system for three related NLP tasks.

    \vspace{0.5em}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{SST:} five-class sentiment classification.
        \item \textbf{Quora Question Pairs:} binary paraphrase detection.
        \item \textbf{STS-B:} semantic textual similarity regression.
    \end{itemize}

    \vspace{0.7em}
    The same checkpoint is also used in a student-support demo: detect sentiment, identify duplicate questions, and retrieve relevant handbook entries.
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{2. Why Multitask Learning?}
    \begin{columns}[T]
        \begin{column}{0.50\textwidth}
            \begin{block}{Training separately}
                \begin{itemize}
                    \item Each task learns its own representation.
                    \item STS has less data and is harder to stabilize.
                    \item Similar sentence-pair signals are not shared.
                \end{itemize}
            \end{block}
        \end{column}
        \begin{column}{0.46\textwidth}
            \begin{alertblock}{Our approach}
                Use one shared BERT encoder with task-specific heads. Paraphrase and STS additionally share a relational layer because both compare sentence pairs.
            \end{alertblock}
        \end{column}
    \end{columns}
\end{frame}

\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{5. Rich Relational Layer}
    Paraphrase and STS both compare sentence pairs, so they share an intermediate interaction layer.

    \[
        z = \frac{h_u \odot h_v}{\|h_u\|_2\|h_v\|_2+\epsilon}
    \]

    \begin{itemize}
        \item $h_u, h_v$: pooled BERT vectors for two sentences.
        \item Element-wise interaction keeps more information than scalar cosine similarity.
        \item The shared layer helps STS benefit from the larger Quora paraphrase dataset.
    \end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{7. Self-Implementation Check}
    \begin{block}{We re-coded BERT}
        Full encoder implemented from scratch:
        \begin{itemize}
            \item No \texttt{AutoModel} forward pass.
            \item Max hidden-state diff: $4.29 \times 10^{-6}$.
            \item Self-impl score: \textbf{0.759}.
            \item HF baseline score: 0.761.
        \end{itemize}
    \end{block}

    \vspace{0.5em}
    The custom encoder is numerically faithful and nearly matches the library-backed model.
\end{frame}

\section{Results}

\begin{frame}{8. Evaluation Metric}
    Each task has its own metric:
    \begin{itemize}
        \item SST: accuracy.
        \item Paraphrase: accuracy.
        \item STS: Pearson correlation.
    \end{itemize}

    \vspace{0.6em}
    Aggregate score:
    \[
        S = \frac{\text{SST}_{acc} + \text{Para}_{acc} + (\text{STS}_{r}+1)/2}{3}
    \]

    STS Pearson correlation is normalized from $[-1,1]$ to $[0,1]$ before averaging.
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{9. Main Results}
    \begin{center}
    \small
    \begin{tabular}{lcccc}
    \toprule
    Model & SST & Para & STS & Score \\
    \midrule
    Self-impl AdamW linear 20e & 0.520 & \textbf{0.864} & 0.785 & \textbf{0.759} \\
    HF sanity baseline 10e & \textbf{0.526} & 0.861 & \textbf{0.790} & 0.761 \\
    Self-impl AdamW constant LR & 0.505 & 0.860 & 0.784 & 0.752 \\
    Self-impl Muon safe 20e & 0.523 & 0.851 & 0.750 & 0.750 \\
    Muon high-LR early run & 0.262 & 0.720 & 0.145 & 0.518 \\
    \bottomrule
    \end{tabular}
    \end{center}

    \vspace{0.4em}
    AdamW with linear warmup/decay is the best self-implemented setup. The HF backend is only a sanity baseline, not the main submission path.
\end{frame}

\section{Conclusion}

\begin{frame}{14. Conclusion}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item We re-coded the full BERT encoder.
        \item Max hidden-state diff: $4.29\times10^{-6}$.
        \item Self-impl score: \textbf{0.759}.
        \item HF baseline: 0.761.
        \item AdamW clearly beats Muon.
        \item Muon damages pretrained weights.
        \item Two demos show both benchmark inference and a concrete student-support use case.
    \end{itemize}

    \vspace{0.5em}
    Future work: better STS calibration, dynamic task weighting, and stronger relational sentence-pair features.
\end{frame}
